Simple Omelette Recipe

An easy and quick dish, perfect for any meal. This classic omelette contains beaten eggs cooked to perfection, optionally filled with your choice of cheese, vegetables, or meats.

Preparation time

  • Total:Approximately 10 minutes
  • Preparation: 5 minutes
  • Cooking: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 large eggs
  • 0.3 teaspoons salt
  • 0.3 teaspoons pepper
  • 1 tablespoons butter or oil

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs: In a bowl, beat 2 large eggs with a pinch of 0.3 teaspoons salt and 0.3 teaspoons pepper until well mixed. For a fluffier texture, add a tablespoon of water or milk.
  2. Heat the pan: Place a non-stick frying pan over medium heat and add 1 tablespoons butter or oil.
  3. Cook the omelette: Once the butter is melted and bubbling, pour in the eggs. Tilt the pan to evenly coat the surface.
  4. Add fillings (optional): When the eggs begin to set at the edges but are still slightly runny in the middle, sprinkle your chosen fillings ({0005}) over half of the omelette.
  5. Fold and serve: As the omelette continues to cook, carefully lift one edge and fold over the fillings, then slide it onto a plate. Serve hot, with additional salt and pepper if needed.
